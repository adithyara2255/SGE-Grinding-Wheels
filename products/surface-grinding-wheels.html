Surface Grinding Wheels

Surface grinding wheels are used for precision grinding and finishing of flat surfaces and specialised industrial components.

Applications

  • Machine bed and slide-way grinding
  • Industrial knife grinding
  • Wood-cutting knife grinding
  • Scissor back and edge grinding
  • Pharmaceutical die grinding
  • Vitrified tile die grinding
  • Creepfeed grinding
  • Harvester blade tooth and edge generation
