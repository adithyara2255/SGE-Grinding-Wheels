Offhand Grinding Wheels

Offhand grinding wheels for applications where grinding is performed manually, including stock removal, tool sharpening and weld seam smoothing.

Product Overview

Offhand grinding refers to grinding material or components while they are held by the operator's hand. The catalogue also describes this application as bench and portable grinding.

These wheels are intended for applications involving stock removal, tool sharpening and smoothing of weld seams.

Applications

  • Foundry and fabrication
  • Automotive fuel injection
  • Hand tools
  • General engineering
  • Metal fabrication
  • Agriculture
