Internal Grinding Wheels

SGE Grinding Wheels provides suitable internal grinding wheel solutions using high-performance abrasive grains and bond systems for precision internal grinding applications.

Applications

Internal grinding wheels are used for precision grinding of internal surfaces, bores and profiles in a range of industrial components.

  • Bearing outer ring track grinding
  • Bearing inner ring bore grinding
  • Gear bore and face grinding
  • Connecting rod bore grinding
  • Driveline cage ID and window grinding
