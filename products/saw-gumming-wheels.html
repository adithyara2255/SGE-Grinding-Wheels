Saw Gumming Wheels

Saw gumming wheels designed for the quick resharpening of saw blades used in woodworking and construction applications.

Product Overview

Saw gumming wheels are used to shape and re-sharpen various types of saws, primarily in the timber and woodworking industries.

The catalogue also identifies applications in construction and saw manufacturing.

Applications

  • Saw blade resharpening
  • Timber industry
  • Woodworking industry
  • Construction
  • Saw manufacturing
