Sol Gel Wheels

Sol Gel Wheels use ceramic abrasive technology designed for cooler cutting and durable grinding performance. They are commonly used for processing steels, hard alloys and other demanding materials.

Applications

Sol Gel Wheels are suited for applications where controlled grinding, profile retention and surface integrity are important.

  • Bearing industry applications
  • Tool room grinding
  • Processing of steels
  • Hard alloy grinding
  • Special material grinding applications
