Depressed Centre (DC) Wheels

Depressed Centre wheels are designed for applications requiring stock removal, grinding and fabrication work. The catalogue highlights options for different materials and industrial requirements.

Product Overview

The catalogue describes DC wheels for applications such as deburring, weld levelling, edge preparation, removal of rolling cracks, cutting runners and risers, V-grooving and chamfering.

The range includes wheels intended for different materials and applications, including stainless steel, high-alloy steel, ferrous metals, cast iron and general grinding work.
