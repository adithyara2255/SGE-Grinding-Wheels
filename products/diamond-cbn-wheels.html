Usage of Diamond and CBN Wheels

Diamond and CBN wheels are selected according to the material being ground and the grinding application. The catalogue identifies Diamond (D) and CBN (CB) for different material groups.

Diamond (D)

Diamond is generally used for grinding non-ferrous materials and other applications listed below.

  • Solid Carbide
  • Oxide Ceramics
  • Glass
  • Stone
  • Porcelain and Stone Ware
  • Semiconductors
  • Ferrite
  • Stellite – Hard Facing
  • Cast Iron

CBN (CB)

CBN is generally used for grinding ferrous materials and is listed for the following applications.

  • High Speed Steel
  • Tool Steel
  • Heat Treated Steel
  • Chromium Steel
  • Case Hardened Steel
  • Super Alloys
  • Bearing Steel
  • Stainless Steel
  • Inconel
