Snagging Wheels

Snagging wheels are used for rough grinding applications to remove unwanted excess material. They are commonly used in the removal of unwanted metal from castings, surface preparation, painting or plating conditioning, weld seams and other heavy stock removal applications.

Applications

  • Rough grinding
  • Removal of excess material from castings
  • Weld seam removal
  • Surface preparation
  • Painting and plating conditioning
  • Steel and non-ferrous metal grinding
  • Cast iron and chill iron applications

Market Solutions

  • Foundry & Metal Fabrication
  • Welding & Construction
  • Steel Mills
  • Shipyards and Railways
