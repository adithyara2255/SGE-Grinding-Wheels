Segments, Straight Cup & Ring Wheels

SGE Grinding Wheels provides grinding solutions in segment, straight cup and ring wheel configurations for specialized face grinding and precision surface grinding applications.

Applications

These grinding wheel configurations can be used for a variety of industrial face grinding and surface grinding requirements.

  • Connecting rod face grinding
  • Industrial and wood-cutting knife grinding
  • Bearing ring face grinding
  • Tappet face grinding
  • Engine block face grinding
  • Insulating brick face grinding
  • Disc brake grinding
  • Compressor valve plate face grinding
  • HSS tool face grinding
