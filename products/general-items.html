Dressing Sticks

Dressing sticks are available in GBC-20 black silicon carbide grade for rough applications and GW-120 to GW-400 white grades for CBN wheel dressing applications.

Sleep Stones

Sleep stones are available in different forms including triangular, square, round and half-round sticks.
