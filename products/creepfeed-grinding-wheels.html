Creepfeed Grinding Wheels

Specially formulated porous bond systems designed for grinding a variety of materials in demanding creepfeed grinding applications.

Product Overview

Creepfeed grinding is used for applications requiring high stock removal rates, including deep forms and high-speed grinding operations.

The catalogue describes these wheels as suitable for applications involving high material removal rates and reduced grinding temperatures.

Applications

  • Aviation and turbine applications
  • Grinding of difficult-to-machine alloys
  • Deep-form grinding
  • High-speed grinding
  • Turbine blade applications
  • Gear grinding
  • Broaching applications
  • Aerospace applications
