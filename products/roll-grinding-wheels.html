Roll Grinding Wheels

SGE Grinding Wheels provides grinding wheel solutions for roll grinding applications where dimensional accuracy, consistent grinding performance and surface finish are important.

Product Overview

Roll grinding wheels are used for grinding rolls used in industrial processes such as steel rolling mills and paper mills.

The selection of abrasive, grade, structure and bond depends on the roll material, grinding machine and required surface finish.

Applications

  • Steel rolling mills
  • General engineering applications
  • Paper mills
  • Industrial roll grinding
  • Precision roll surface finishing
