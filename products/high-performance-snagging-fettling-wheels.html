High Performance Snagging / Fettling Wheels

SGE Grinding Wheels provides heavy-duty snagging and fettling wheel solutions for demanding casting grinding applications where efficient stock removal is required.

Applications

These wheels are suitable for heavy-duty grinding and fettling of a variety of cast components.

  • Investment casting grinding
  • High-chrome casting grinding
  • Sintered casting grinding
  • Ni-hard casting grinding
  • Manganese steel casting grinding
  • SG iron and grey cast iron casting grinding
  • Cast iron (CI) casting grinding
