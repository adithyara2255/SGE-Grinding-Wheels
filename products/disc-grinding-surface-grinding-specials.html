Disc Grinding – Surface Grinding Specials

Specially designed nut-inserted wheels offering improved control and optimum performance on different materials.

Product Overview

Disc grinding is similar to surface grinding, but provides a larger contact area between the grinding disc and the work piece.

These wheels are available in different forms for demanding grinding applications where dimensional control and surface finishing are important.

Applications

  • Automotive applications
  • Bearing rings
  • Cylindrical rollers
  • Bearing needles
  • Special bearing components
  • General engineering
  • Foundry and fabrication
