Reinforced Cut-Off Wheels

Reinforced cut-off wheels designed for convenient, quick and efficient cutting applications across industrial and fabrication environments.

Product Overview

Reinforced cut-off wheels are used for cutting through metals and other industrial materials where controlled and efficient cutting is required.

The catalogue range is intended for applications including sheet metal cutting, pipe cutting, removal of risers, metal plate dimensioning and rail cutting.

Applications

  • Oil and gas pipeline construction and refinery work
  • Automotive applications
  • Engine valve cutting
  • Steel manufacturing
  • Investment casting
  • Sheet metal cutting
  • Pipe cutting
  • Rail cutting
