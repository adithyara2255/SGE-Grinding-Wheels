Tool Room Grinding Wheels

SGE Grinding Wheels provides tool room grinding wheel solutions in different abrasive grain types, shapes and configurations to suit customer application requirements.

Abrasive Grain Options

The available grain options include:

  • Green Silicon Carbide
  • White Aluminium Oxide
  • Pink Aluminium Oxide
  • Mono-crystalline grain
  • Ceramic grain

Wheel Shapes

Tool room grinding wheels are available in different shapes according to the grinding application.

  • Plain Shape — Type 1
  • Straight Cup Shape — Type 6
  • Taper Cup — Type 11
  • Dish Shape — Type 12
  • Saucer Shape — Type 13
